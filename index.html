How booking works

Booking is by request. Nothing is confirmed until the studio approves it.

  1. 1

    Choose a design

    Pick a design from the catalog, or describe your own idea for custom artwork.

  2. 2

    Send your request

    Tell us your preferred date, time, placement and size. A reference photo helps.

  3. 3

    Wait for confirmation

    The studio reviews your request and replies with confirmation, any deposit amount and the payment deadline.

  4. 4

    Pay and get inked

    Pay the deposit once your booking is approved, then come in for your session.

Booking status

Every request moves through these statuses. Only the studio can mark an appointment as confirmed.

  • Request SubmittedYou send the form.
  • Awaiting ConfirmationThe studio is reviewing it.
  • ConfirmedApproved by the studio, with deposit details.
  • RescheduledA new date was agreed.
  • CancelledThe booking was cancelled.
